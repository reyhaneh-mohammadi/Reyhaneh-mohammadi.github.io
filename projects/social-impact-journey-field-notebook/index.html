A Social Impact Journey — Field Notebook

Turning Urban Exploration into a Personal Field Journal

Client
ESCP Business School, Spain
Role
Editorial Design · Zine Design · Visual Communication

01 Project Overview

As part of A Social Impact Journey, a workshop-based learning experience developed for ESCP Business School in Madrid, I designed a printed Field Notebook to accompany students during their exploration of different neighbourhoods.

The notebook combined cultural and social information, guided activities, and space for personal observations. I chose a zine-inspired format to create a more engaging and personal alternative to a traditional academic workbook.

02 The Challenge

The challenge was to transform information-rich educational content into an engaging, easy-to-follow publication that encouraged active participation.

Rather than creating a conventional workbook, I wanted the notebook to feel personal and inviting, helping students engage with the material, reflect on their experiences, and document their discoveries without feeling overwhelmed by instructions.

03 The Approach

I structured the Field Notebook around a consistent two-page layout, dedicating each spread to a specific topic.

The left page presented contextual information, questions, and activities, while the right page provided space for students to write, mark, and record their observations.

This clear structure separated reading from responding, making the notebook intuitive to navigate.

I used a blue-and-yellow colour palette, typography, and graphic elements consistent with the accompanying neighbourhood maps to create a cohesive visual identity across the printed materials.

04 The Outcome

The Field Notebook was printed and used at ESCP Business School as part of the urban exploration workshop.

Designed as a companion to the neighbourhood maps, it provided students with a structured space to engage with the content, complete activities, and record or mark their observations.

Together, the two printed tools supported different parts of the workshop experience: exploring the neighbourhoods and reflecting on what students discovered.
