Data Discovery

Making the Design Process Visible

Client
Ministerie van Financiën
Academic Partner
Hogeschool van Amsterdam
Role
Visual Storytelling · Editorial Illustration · Digital Communication
Link to project
digitalsocietyschool.org/project/data-discovery

01 Project Overview

As part of Data Discovery, a collaborative project developing a physical facilitation tool to improve communication around data for the Dutch Ministry of Finance, I took the lead in documenting and visually communicating our design journey.

Throughout each sprint, I captured key moments through notes and quick sketches, later transforming them into accessible narratives and minimal, conceptual illustrations. My aim was to help audiences unfamiliar with the project follow our journey as if they had been part of it.

02 The Challenge

The project followed a nonlinear, exploratory process. Through multiple prototypes, experiments, and iterations, we gradually developed a clearer understanding of a complex problem.

The challenge was to communicate this journey without overwhelming the audience or losing the connections between different stages. Rather than creating another academic-style report, I wanted to make the team's trials, discoveries, and evolving thinking accessible and engaging, placing the process itself at the heart of the story.

03 The Approach

I approached each sprint as a chapter in an unfolding story, using a narrative writing style to make the team's journey engaging and easy to follow.

To introduce the project, I opened with a deliberately confusing question, inviting readers to experience the uncertainty that often surrounds conversations about data. This became the starting point for the story.

Throughout the process, I selected key moments from each sprint and translated them into minimal, conceptual illustrations, featuring the people involved in the project as characters in the narrative.

Each illustration served as a standalone visual summary, allowing the audience to grasp the essence of a sprint at a glance.

04 The Outcome

The university’s project webpage brought together the written sprint narratives and seven conceptual illustrations. The illustrations received positive feedback and were selected for print display at the final showcase, where I created the seventh illustration live.

I also designed a poster to spark visitors’ curiosity and draw them to the Data Discovery booth. The poster was printed and installed at the showcase entrance.
