A Social Impact Journey

Designing Printed Tools for Urban Cultural Exploration and Learning

Client
ESCP Business School, Spain
Role
Graphic Design · Editorial Design · Visual Identity

01 Project Overview

A Social Impact Journey is a workshop-based learning experience developed for ESCP Business School in Madrid, inviting students to explore different neighbourhoods through guided activities and cultural discovery.

I designed a cohesive set of printed materials, including foldable neighbourhood maps and a Field Notebook in zine format. Together, these tools combined local information, visual guidance, and interactive exercises to support students throughout their exploration.

My role covered the visual identity, editorial design, and layout of both printed formats.

02 The Challenge

The challenge was to transform dense cultural and social content into engaging, easy-to-navigate printed materials without compromising readability or usability.

For the foldable maps, finding the right format and layout was essential to balancing information density with practical use. The Field Notebook presented a different challenge: turning structured learning activities into something more personal and inviting, encouraging students to explore, reflect, and document their discoveries rather than simply complete an academic assignment.

03 The Approach

I explored different paper sizes and folding techniques to create a compact, pocket-friendly map that balanced rich content with ease of use.

I treated each folded panel as an individual editorial layout while maintaining consistency across the entire map. A bold blue-and-yellow palette, clear typography, and illustrated icons helped organise dense information and guide exploration. I also developed a reusable map template that could be adapted to different neighbourhoods through their content and visual details.

For the Field Notebook, I chose a zine format with a consistent two-page structure: information and prompts on the left, and space for students' observations on the right. A shared visual language connected both printed tools into one cohesive experience.

04 The Outcome

The project resulted in five foldable neighbourhood maps built around a reusable visual template, alongside a complementary Field Notebook.

All materials were printed and used at ESCP Business School as part of the learning experience. Feedback highlighted how the presentation of information could support student engagement and learning.

The map template was also retained for use in future projects, extending the design beyond its initial application.
