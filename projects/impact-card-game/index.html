Impact Card Game

Designing Cards for an Interactive Learning Experience

Client
ESCP Business School, Spain
Role
Card Design · Graphic Design · Visual Communication

01 Project Overview

Impact Card Game is a card-based learning activity developed for students at ESCP Business School in Spain, encouraging discussion around social, economic, cultural, and urban topics through a playful format.

I was responsible for the visual design of the card deck, creating a cohesive graphic system across five categories: Urban Space, Platform Governance, Economic, Social & Community Relations, and Jokers.

The project explored how graphic design could support interaction, clarity, and engagement within an educational card game.

02 The Challenge

The challenge was to translate diverse social, economic, and cultural topics into a cohesive set of visually engaging cards while maintaining readability within a limited space.

Each category needed a distinct visual identity to help students recognise and navigate the cards easily, while the overall design had to remain consistent with the visual language established in my previous project for ESCP Business School.

Balancing information, illustration, and usability was essential to creating an intuitive and engaging learning tool.

03 The Approach

I developed a visual system that made each card category easily recognisable through a dedicated colour and icon, while maintaining consistency across the entire deck.

I divided each card into two sections, keeping the main content at the centre and the category label at the bottom. Typography, colour, and illustrations were aligned with the visual language of my previous project for ESCP Business School.

Following an initial playtest, I refined the design by increasing the card size and introducing clearer colour coding to improve readability and usability.

04 The Outcome

The final outcome was a printed deck of 30 cards across five categories, designed as a cohesive visual system for classroom use.

The cards were printed and used at ESCP Business School, then further refined based on feedback from use. Increasing the card size improved usability, while the revised colour coding made category differences clearer and easier to navigate.

The final version was approved and prepared for final print production.
